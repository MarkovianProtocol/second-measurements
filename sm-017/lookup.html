SECOND MEASUREMENT SM-017 · STATION LOOKUP

Did your station post its quarterly list on time?

Type a US broadcast call sign (KAAA, WNBC, KRHD-CD). The card shows the earliest upload date we found for each quarter's issues/programs list in the station's FCC public file, against the deadline, plus the licensee and owner from the FCC's licensing database. Read 14 October 2026, with files our 6–10 October read saw that have since been removed added back, so each list shows the earliest date we saw; the paper and method are at sm-017.html.

"Late" is an upload date after the deadline (the tenth of the month after the quarter; the next business day when the tenth is a weekend or holiday; 1 December 2025 for the shutdown quarter). "No list found" means no file in the Issues and Programs Lists folder that we could read as that quarter's list; the station may have named it in a way the script did not recognise, put it in another folder, or not have one. Translators, low-power TV and unbuilt permits keep no list and are not here. Data: lookup.json, built by make_lookup.py from the raw reads in the exhibits.
