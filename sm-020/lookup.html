SECOND MEASUREMENT SM-020 · LOBBYING LOOKUP

Did this lobbying firm, or this client’s lobbyist, report on time?

Type a lobbying firm or a client. The card shows every quarterly report the firm posted after the 20-day deadline from January 2025 to July 2026, and, for the 20 October 2026 deadline, whether each of its clients from the previous quarter had a report up by midnight. Data from lda.gov, the Senate and House lobbying disclosure site; the paper and method are at sm-020.html. For foreign governments’ agents under FARA, see the country lookup (SM-024).

A report is late when lda.gov’s posted time, in Eastern time, falls after the deadline day (the 20th day after the quarter, or the next business day). Only late reports are listed one by one; a firm with none here filed every report on time in that period, or did not file. The 20 October column covers firm–client pairs that filed a regular or no-activity report for April–June 2026 and did not end the registration; “nothing posted” means no report for July–September was on lda.gov by midnight, read on 21 October. A firm can be late for a reason the data does not show. Data: lookup.json, built by build_lookup.py from the exhibits.
