This is Chainflip Labs. On 21 November 2026 you took 122.86137042 BTC, 520.2243399 ETH, 3,249,631.41 USDC and 758,141.33 USDT from the Chainflip vaults.

We have identified the mechanism of key exposure and have prevented such future attacks. Your addresses are flagged with the major blockchain analytics firms, which will make it extremely difficult to liquidate the proceeds of your actions, and every withdrawal from the Tornado Cash pools you used is being watched.
