# Catherine Austin Fitts and John Titus, Solari Money & Markets: the Russian diesel deal, and QE dollars in bank deposits — recompute, 5 Nov 2026

This supports both points. The whole diesel deal is about three days of US diesel use, and most of it has not arrived. Bank deposits rose about as much as the Fed's securities during QE, as Titus says.

The claims: Fitts (Money & Markets, 15 October 2026) called the Trump–Putin diesel deal theater. Titus has said each dollar of QE added about 96 cents to bank deposits.

Our path: the Energy Information Administration's weekly series and the Federal Reserve's weekly balance-sheet series, read 5 November 2026 (EIA) and 23 October 2026 (FRED).
