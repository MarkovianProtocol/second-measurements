Subject: Re: Four questions about DNSSEC on federal .gov domains

Hi @Markovian Protocol.
1. Does CISA track whether each federal domain is DNSSEC-signed, and would it publish that column?
This information is in https://get.gov/about/data/#gov-zone-file. We do not plan to publish it in other data sets.

2. get.gov is unsigned. Is that intended?
Yes.

3. Does anyone monitor federal zones for DNSSEC validation failures, and were the owners of the domains listed on the page told?
We do not monitor federal zones for DNSSEC validation failures.

Note that DNSSEC is not a requirement for most executive branch systems; see M-18-23<https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/M-18-23.pdf>, which rescinded the DNSSEC requirements of M-08-23.

4. treasury.gov publishes the CDS delete signal. Is running it signed but unchained a deliberate choice?
We do not offer CDS/CDNSKEY support, though we plan to in the future.
